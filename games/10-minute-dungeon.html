DUNGEON GUARDIAN

10-MINUTE
DUNGEON

HOW TO PLAY

WASD – move
MOUSE – aim
HOLD LEFT CLICK – sword swing
SPACE – dash (invulnerable)
ESC / P – pause

Arcane bolts fire on their own at the nearest foe. Collect purple crystals to level up and choose upgrades. Open chests, drink potions, and survive until the Dungeon Guardian awakens at 9:00 – then slay it.
