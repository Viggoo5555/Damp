Hedgerow Hollow

Guard the veggie patch from snails, beetles and one very rude badger.

These are the critters that walk the path. Every one that reaches the patch eats a veggie.

How to play

Pick a buddy, then tap a patch of grass to plant it. Tap a planted buddy and a box pops up right over it with two upgrade paths.

Press Send wave when you are ready, or switch on Auto. Rain, Thunder and Sunshine help in a tight spot.

Win rounds to earn golden seeds. Spend them in the Shop on locked buddies; skins are sold by the mystery trader, who wanders into the garden with a new stock every hour. The Daily garden changes every day and keeps a streak. The Endless meadow never ends.

Every third wave a hedgehog trader stops by with a trade. Each garden also has two goals that pay golden seeds the first time you meet them.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD move the cursor, 1 to 9, 0, - and = pick a buddy, Enter plants, U and I buy upgrades, X sells, N sends the wave, Q E R use powers, T toggles Auto, P pauses, F changes speed.

Sound starts after your first tap. Progress is saved in this browser.

How to play

  1. Pick a buddy in the bar, then tap the glowing grass to plant it.
  2. Tap a planted buddy. A box pops up right over it with two upgrade paths.
  3. A green arrow over a buddy means you can afford an upgrade.
  4. Press Send wave. Critters follow the arrows to the veggie patch.
  5. Rain, Thunder and Sunshine help in a tight spot.
  6. Now and then a mystery trader wanders onto the grass. Tap him to buy skins with golden seeds, or to trade seeds for an upgrade, a seedling or sharper teeth.
  7. Tap the corner button for full screen: a bigger map with a buddy strip you can scroll.
