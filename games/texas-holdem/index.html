    The house rules

    How to play

    No-limit Texas Hold'em

    Everyone gets two private cards. Five community cards are dealt face up in the middle: the flop, the turn, then the river. Make the best five-card hand from any combination of the seven.

    Two forced bets start each hand — the small blind and the big blind. The player to the left of the big blind acts first before the flop. After the flop, action starts to the left of the dealer.

    The four actions

    • CheckPass when there is nothing to call.
    • CallMatch the current bet.
    • Bet / RaisePut more in. The minimum raise is the size of the last raise.
    • FoldGive up the hand and any chips already in the pot.

    If you run out of chips in a hand, you are all-in. A side pot is created so the other players can keep betting. At showdown, the best hand in each pot takes it.
