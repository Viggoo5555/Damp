How to play

Pick a buddy, then tap a patch of grass to plant it. Tap a planted buddy to open its two upgrade paths. Send each wave when you are ready, or switch on Auto. Powers help in a tight spot. Every critter that reaches the patch eats a veggie.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD move the cursor, 1 to 6 pick a buddy, Enter plants, U and I buy upgrades, X sells, N sends the wave, Q E R use powers, T toggles Auto, P pauses, F changes speed.

Sound starts after your first tap. Progress is saved in this browser.
